From the power button to the login screen

Boot Sequence

What PCs, servers and virtual machines do between pressing the power button and being ready. BIOS and UEFI, Windows, Linux and macOS, boot order rules, disk layouts and timings, from a plain-language overview to the CPU modes, keys and kernel internals underneath.

Timings are illustrative: they vary widely with hardware, settings and configuration. File paths and commands are the common defaults on current systems.

Message sequences are simplified to the steps that matter for comparison. Real firmware and boot loaders do much more.
