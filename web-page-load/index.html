The classic interview question, taken apart

From Enter to Pixels

You type google.com and press Enter. Roughly half a second later the page is on screen. Step through the 26 things that happen in between, and switch views at any point without losing your place.

Addresses such as 192.168.1.23, 203.0.113.7 and 198.51.100.53 come from private or documentation ranges. 142.250.74.36 stands in for whatever Google address your resolver returns. Root, .com and ns1.google.com addresses are the real published ones.

Timings are illustrative for a cold start on home broadband. Real numbers vary a lot, and a browser that already has a warm connection skips most of stages 2 to 4.
